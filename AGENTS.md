# CAFEBRARY Agent Instructions

## Project purpose
CAFEBRARY is a warm, editorial café and library experience built around quiet hospitality, books, coffee, and belonging. The brand is grounded in comfort, thoughtful pacing, and a strong café-first identity.

## Brand identity
- Café first, library second: 60% café / 40% library.
- Tagline: "Between the pages and the pour, you're home!"
- Brand promise: "We keep your place."
- Mission: "Make a quiet hour easy to have."
- Brand personality: The Quiet Friend.
- Writing voice: understated, human, warm, plain, thoughtful, and welcoming.
- Avoid generic AI marketing phrasing, exaggerated claims, promotional hype, and unnecessary flourish.
- Never describe CAFEBRARY as fictional, imaginary, a demo, or a portfolio project in website content.

## Approved visual system
### Color palette
- Caramel: #C68642
- Dark Navy: #1F2D44
- Dusty Rose: #B67C8A
- Dark Cocoa: #3E2A1F
- Latte Foam: #F4E7D3

Keep the hierarchy intentional and readable. Preserve brand proportions and the existing warm editorial palette. Use contrast carefully for accessible readability.

### Typography
- Logo: Bogale (logo only)
- Headings: Sanrello
- Body: The Seasons
- Accent typography: Abril Fatface

Use graceful fallbacks if these typefaces are unavailable. Do not claim fonts are installed if they are not loaded.

### Visual direction
Modern Heritage / European heritage café.

Favor warm golden café atmosphere, timeless editorial layout, natural materials, architectural character, quiet elegance, thoughtful whitespace, and refined typography.

WORKSPACE should remain distinctive: neutral, daylight-driven, clear, productive, calm, and restrained. It must not look futuristic or technology-themed.

## Content principles
- Preserve the distinction between BOOKHOUSE, WORKSPACE, and BOOKMARK.
- Keep copy grounded in real, human café language.
- Prioritize clarity, warmth, and restraint over decorative wording.
- If an approved menu item or business detail is uncertain, preserve the existing item rather than inventing a replacement.
- Do not introduce generic AI filler or repetitive phrasing.

## Design and technical constraints
- Preserve the existing React + Vite architecture and custom router.
- Do not rebuild the app from scratch or replace the current structure.
- Do not install unnecessary dependencies.
- Do not remove useful existing components or routes.
- Keep image placeholders in place until dedicated image integration work is approved.
- Maintain the existing seven-page structure and intended page purposes.
- Prefer refinement over broad refactoring.

## Development rules
- Be conservative with changes: preserve working interactions and design intent.
- Keep the site premium, editorial, and welcoming rather than generic or over-styled.
- Maintain responsive behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Improve consistency in spacing, hierarchy, contrast, and card treatments without flattening the brand personality.
- Keep navigation, buttons, and information architecture consistent across all pages.
- Preserve approved menu content and established brand language unless an explicit correction is required.

## Quality bar
The final result should feel like a finished café brand site: elegant, warm, readable, and grounded in the quiet rhythm of real coffee and reading culture.
