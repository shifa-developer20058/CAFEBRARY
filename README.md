# CAFEBRARY

**Between the pages and the pour, you're home.**

A café with a little more room for books, quieter moments, and the things we never seem to find time for.

---

## About the Brand

CAFEBRARY is built around a simple idea: a café should be somewhere you can stay, not just somewhere you stop.

Bringing together the familiar warmth of a café and the quiet comfort of a reading space, CAFEBRARY makes room for people who want to read, think, write, work, or simply spend an unhurried hour away from the everyday noise.

Coffee comes first. Books make it better. And there's no particular reason to rush.

## What Makes CAFEBRARY Different

### BOOKHOUSE
A shared reading library within the café. Visitors can explore the collection, pick up a book, and continue reading at their own pace. The books remain at CAFEBRARY, ready for the next reader.

### WORKSPACE
A dedicated silent room for uninterrupted focus. Designed for those who need a little distance from conversations, notifications, and distractions, with membership and seat reservations.

### BOOKMARK
A reading passport for keeping track of the books and pages that matter to you. A small way of making it easier to return to where you left off.

### The Café
A welcoming menu of coffee, beverages, and desserts, served in an atmosphere designed to make staying a little longer feel natural.

## Website

The CAFEBRARY website brings the brand, its spaces, and its offerings together in one place. It is designed to introduce the experience, help visitors understand how everything works, and make planning a visit straightforward.

### Pages

- **Home** — An introduction to CAFEBRARY and its atmosphere.
- **Our Story** — The thinking, purpose, and identity behind the café.
- **Settle In** — A closer look at the experience of spending time at CAFEBRARY.
- **Menu** — Coffee, beverages, and desserts.
- **Bookhouse** — Information about the shared reading library.
- **Workspace** — Details about the silent focus room and membership.
- **Visit** — Practical information for planning a visit.

## Design Direction

CAFEBRARY takes inspiration from European café heritage, warm interiors, old libraries, and the feeling of returning somewhere familiar.

The visual identity combines:

- Warm caramel and latte tones
- Deep navy and dark cocoa
- Muted dusty rose accents
- Heritage-inspired typography and imagery
- A balance of traditional character and contemporary web design

The intention is not to recreate the past, but to make something that feels as though it has always belonged.

## Getting Started

To run the website locally:

```bash
git clone <repository-url>
cd Project_CAFEBRARY
npm install
npm run dev
```

To create a production build:

```bash
npm run build
```

The production output is generated in the `dist` directory.
